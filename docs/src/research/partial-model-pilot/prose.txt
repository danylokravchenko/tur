The museum moved its opening time from ten in the morning to nine during the summer. Staff discovered that visitors arriving by train often reached the entrance before the doors opened. The new schedule gave those visitors time to explore the permanent collection before the first guided tour.

Attendance increased on weekdays, while weekend attendance stayed roughly the same. The director did not attribute the entire increase to the schedule change. A new exhibition and a reduced student ticket price began during the same month. Comparing several weeks of records could describe the association, but separating the causes would require a more careful study.

The report recommended keeping the earlier opening time through September. It also proposed recording arrival times and asking visitors how they learned about the museum. These changes would help staff plan future schedules with better evidence.
